Deviation Reporting Axis — Help
What angle should I enter?
Enter the azimuth (compass bearing) of your structure's main axis, measured clockwise from TRUE North. Range 0–360° (0° = North, 90° = East, 180° = South, 270° = West).
What you will get
  • Along-axis — movement in the direction of the axis you enter (e.g. lengthwise along a dam crest, rail, wall, or slope).
  • Transverse-axis — movement perpendicular to it (90° clockwise), usually the critical direction (e.g. across a dam: upstream/downstream).
How to obtain the angle
  • Design drawing / site plan: read the bearing of the structure's main axis.
  • Map (e.g. Google Earth): draw a line along the axis and read its bearing.
  • Two survey points on the axis: azimuth = atan2(ΔEast, ΔNorth), converted to 0–360°.
  • Compass: read the magnetic bearing, then add the local magnetic declination to get the TRUE bearing.
Example
A dam running North-East to South-West → enter 45. “Along” then reports movement along the dam, and “Transverse” reports movement across it.
Leave the checkbox unticked to report standard East / North / Up. The total movement magnitude is identical in either mode — only the axis direction changes.
